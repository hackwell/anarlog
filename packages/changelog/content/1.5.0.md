## Everything stays on your Mac

- Record, transcribe and write notes without an account — there is no sign-in,
  and nothing to sign in to
- No usage tracking and no crash reporting: the app opens no outbound
  connection of its own
- Your notes, transcripts and recordings live in a local database and never
  leave the machine unless you export them

## Microsoft 365 calendar

- Connect an Outlook or Microsoft 365 calendar directly, through your own
  Microsoft account — read-only, and without any service in between
- Meeting times now arrive in the timezone Microsoft actually reports them in,
  including all-day events and meetings that span a daylight-saving change
- Apple Calendar keeps working exactly as before

## A clearer sidebar

- Meetings and notes appear as cards showing duration and recording state, not
  just a title and a time
- Search notes and meetings straight from the sidebar
- The "now" marker is labelled instead of appearing as an unexplained line
- The sidebar carries the brand blue, so navigation and your notes are told
  apart at a glance
- Red is reserved for one thing: audio is being captured

## German, properly

- Clock and date formats follow the language you picked — 24-hour times and
  German month names throughout
- The interface has been translated in full

## Also in this release

- New app, menu bar and window icons drawn from the Session Echo mark
- Updates are signed with Session Echo's own key and delivered from its own
  release channel
