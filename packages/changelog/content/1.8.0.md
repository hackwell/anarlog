## Slides the summary can actually read

- Captured slides are read on your Mac and their text goes to the summary, so
  even a model that cannot see images knows what was on screen
- Only what changed on a slide is handed over, so a deck of near-identical
  slides no longer repeats itself in the summary
- Browser meetings are cropped to the page content: no tab bar, no bookmarks,
  no address bar in the picture
- A slide is kept when its text is new, not when pixels moved, so a running
  timer, a moving cursor or a speaking head no longer counts as a new slide
- Frames are stored as WebP and are a fraction of their former size
- The 60-slide limit now counts per session, so stopping and resuming a
  recording keeps every slide visible

## Who was in the Teams call

- Session Echo can read the names on the video tiles of a Teams call and attach
  them as participants, looking them up in your contacts and creating them when
  they are new
- Your own tile is skipped, and a participant you remove stays removed
- Off by default: Settings › General › Meetings, next to chat capture. It needs
  Accessibility access

## Transcribing looks like transcribing

- While a recording is being transcribed, the transcript view draws the
  recording's waveform and fills it as the work progresses, with the position in
  minutes and a breathing bar where it currently is — instead of a spinner and
  a number

## Also in this release

- Screen Recording has its own row under Settings › Permissions, and turning on
  slide capture asks for it right away
- Screen Recording is now read where the app runs, so a permission you granted
  is no longer reported as missing
- The slide strip above your notes scrolls when there are more slides than fit
