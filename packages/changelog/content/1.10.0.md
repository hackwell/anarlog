## Every meeting knows its customer

- A meeting shows the customer it belongs to, right next to the folder in its
  header
- When someone in the meeting is already one of your contacts at a company, the
  customer is filled in by itself
- When only their email domain matches a company you know, you get a suggestion
  to confirm — a guess is never applied on its own
- Meetings among your own colleagues stay internal and are left alone
- Where two companies sit in one meeting, the one with more people in the room
  wins, and a tie goes to the customer you worked with most recently
- Customer names go into the search index, so a customer's name finds their
  meetings
- Got one wrong? "No customer" clears it, and it stays cleared

## Before it can fill anything in

- Settings › General has a new field for your own email domains. Until it is
  filled, every meeting looks external, so the app only ever suggests — it
  never assigns
- It is filled from your own email address on first start where the app knows it
