## Your notes remember where you were

- A line you type while recording keeps the point in the recording it was
  written at, and shows that time at the end of the line
- Click the time to jump the playback there, so a half-finished thought takes
  you back to what was actually said around it
- The times go into the summary as well, so the model can connect a note line
  to that part of the conversation instead of reading the two as unrelated
  texts
- Lines in bullet lists carry their time into the summary too
- It works whether or not live transcription is on, since the position comes
  from the recording itself
- Older notes are untouched: no times, and nothing about them changes
