## Two halves, not one list

- The sidebar has two tabs at the bottom. **Recordings** is where it opens: only
  what you captured, newest first, without the calendar burying it
- **Timeline** is the day ahead, reading downward the way a day does, with a
  marked line for right now
- The next meeting stays visible in both, so reading your notes never means
  missing it
- Rows tell each other apart at a glance: a recording carries a mark because it
  holds something, and a calendar entry with nobody else in it steps back

## Finding a recording again

- Filter by the people who were in it. Nothing to maintain — participants come
  with the calendar invite
- Give recordings tags, and filter by those. Existing tags are suggested while
  you type, so one idea does not end up split across two words
- Typing in the search narrows the filters too: search a name, pick it, and you
  have every recording with that person

## Tags the app proposes

- After a meeting, Session Echo can read the transcript and suggest tags. It
  chooses from the tags you already use rather than inventing new words, and
  marks a genuinely new one so you decide
- Suggested, never applied on its own
- The transcript can stay on your Mac for this: a local model handles it

## Menu bar

- The menu shows the whole day instead of the next three entries, each with its
  full time span
- It speaks the language the app is set to

## Also in this release

- One setting decides whether times read as 12 or 24 hours, everywhere —
  including exported notes, which used to be stuck on English and US format
- A gear in the sidebar opens settings
