## Long meetings keep their words

- A recording whose transcript stalled once could finish with the words held
  back instead of saved into the meeting. The summary then refused to run —
  "Not enough words recorded" — on a meeting whose transcript was right there
  on screen. The saving step now always gets its own attempt, and retries
  before it gives up
- Anything still left over is repaired quietly in the background, at startup
  and every few minutes. A crash, a forced quit or a flat battery no longer
  leaves a meeting stranded
- Until that repair runs, the summary, the chat and everything else already
  read the full transcript, so a meeting is never summarized from half of what
  was said

## Find a meeting by its customer

- Searching for a customer's name in the sidebar now finds the meetings
  assigned to them, not just meetings with that name in the title
- A locked meeting keeps its customer to itself
