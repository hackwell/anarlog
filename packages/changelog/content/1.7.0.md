## Slides from the meeting window

- While you record, Session Echo can screenshot **only the meeting window** —
  Zoom, Teams, Meet and the rest, native or in the browser — and keeps a frame
  only when what is shown actually changes. No full-screen captures, nothing
  from other windows
- Kept slides appear as thumbnails above your note; click one to put it in.
  The summary sees them too when your model can read images
- Off by default: Settings › General › Meetings › *Capture slides from the
  meeting window*. It needs Screen Recording access

## Meetings that continue

- A **History** tab shows earlier occurrences of the same meeting — same
  series, same title or the same people — with their key facts and the full
  summary on demand, readable while the current one is still running
- The summary of a recurring meeting now knows the last three occurrences and
  says what got resolved since, what stays open and what is new

## Tags and copying, where you look for them

- Tags sit in the session header as chips. After a summary finishes, Session
  Echo suggests tags for recordings that have none yet; accept or dismiss
  with one click
- A copy button in the header copies whatever is open — summary, note or
  transcript

## Menu bar and mini window

- A calendar entry in the menu opens a small menu instead of starting a
  recording straight away: start recording, join and record, prepare a note,
  or open the meeting link
- The menu is leaner (no version line, update check or hide entry) and has
  icons. Showing events in the menu bar is now a switch in Settings › General
- While recording, the sound waves in the menu bar logo pulse instead of the
  bars beside it
- The floating recording window has a button that brings Session Echo to the
  front, and shows the meeting title as a ticker when you hover

## Also in this release

- The transcription model picker marks models that tell speakers apart
- Live transcription with OpenAI's `gpt-live-transcribe` works again
- The sidebar switches views with a sliding pill, uses a lighter blue, and no
  longer shows a "now" line in the Recordings view
