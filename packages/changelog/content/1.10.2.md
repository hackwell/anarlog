## Long recordings go through again

- Re-transcribing a recording longer than about 25 minutes failed with "audio
  duration is longer than 1400 seconds". A long recording is sent in pieces, and
  the pieces were cut slightly larger than the transcription model accepts.
  They are shorter now, so the whole recording goes through

## The app tells us when something breaks

- Session Echo now reports its own errors, so a failure no longer depends on
  someone noticing it and describing it from memory
- Reports go to a server in Germany. They carry what broke and where in the app
  it happened — never your recordings, notes, summaries or transcripts
- Names, email addresses, IP addresses, your computer's name and anything under
  your home folder are removed before a report is sent
